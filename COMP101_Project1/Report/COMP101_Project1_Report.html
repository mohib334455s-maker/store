1. Introduction

This project implements a complete English-language e-commerce website named Northlane Outfitters. The store sells premium outdoor equipment organized into three product categories: Hiking, Camping, and Travel. The catalog contains exactly ten products. Each product includes a unique SKU, title, category, numerical price, numerical stock quantity, professional English description, and an online ImageURL.

The selected platform is Northlane Commerce (Next.js e-commerce platform), an accepted alternative to Wix Stores / Shopify for COMP101. It provides the required storefront, CSV product import, inventory tracking, test checkout, order fulfillment, and tracking-number workflow. The brand identity uses a minimal circular NL mark with stacked Northlane Outfitters wordmark.

Product images are hosted online and referenced through ImageURL values in the CSV. Inventory tracking is enabled. The Compact Trail First Aid Kit (NL-TRV-002) was prepared with stock quantity 1, purchased through Manual Payment in test mode, reduced to stock 0, and displayed as Out of Stock. The test order was fulfilled with tracking number DXB-TRK-12345.

2. Development Process

  1. Branding. Store concept defined as a premium outdoor outfitter.
  2. Logo. Circular NL mark with stacked Northlane Outfitters wordmark applied across header, footer, dashboard, report, and presentation.
  3. Storefront styling. Consistent navigation, buttons, and admin actions applied across the site.
  4. CSV creation. Official headers SKU,Title,Category,Price,Stock,Description,ImageURL used for 10 products.
  5. Online images. Direct online image links placed in ImageURL. No local file paths.
  6. CSV import. All products uploaded through the dashboard import process.
  7. Inventory tracking. Stock quantities tracked and reduced during checkout.
  8. Out-of-Stock test. NL-TRV-002 set to 1, purchased, then shown as Out of Stock.
  9. Fulfillment. Order NL-10001 marked shipped with tracking DXB-TRK-12345.
  10. Mobile testing. Storefront verified on phone-width layout.
